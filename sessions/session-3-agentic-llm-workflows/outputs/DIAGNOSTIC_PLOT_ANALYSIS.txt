ANSWER: BEST SUPPORTING PLOT

**The Factor Boxplots (part2_mofa_factor_boxplots.png) most directly support the 
claim that MOFA captures breast-cancer subtype biology.**


WHY THIS PLOT IS THE STRONGEST EVIDENCE
─────────────────────────────────────────

1. VISUAL STRATIFICATION
   Factor2 shows clear spatial separation of subtypes along a single latent axis:
   - Basal tumors occupy the extreme negative region
   - Luminal subtypes occupy positive/neutral regions
   - This visualization directly demonstrates that the MOFA latent space 
     encodes the biological distinctions between intrinsic subtypes

2. STATISTICAL STRENGTH (η² = 0.7426)
   - 74% of Factor2's variance is explained by subtype group membership
   - This is exceptionally high (values > 0.14 are considered strong)
   - Shows Factor2 is not just any latent factor, but one fundamentally 
     organized by subtype biology

3. DIRECT INTERPRETATION
   - Boxplots show the actual MOFA output (latent factors, Z)
   - Directly stratified by the PAM50 classification (the ground truth)
   - No intermediate model required: factors speak for themselves
   - Contrasts with confusion matrix, which requires fitting a classifier

4. BIOLOGICAL MEANING
   - The clear Basal ←→ Luminal separation along Factor2 maps directly to 
     known breast cancer biology:
     * Basal-like tumors are characterized by stem-cell-like features
     * Luminal A tumors are characterized by hormone receptor expression
     * These are fundamentally different transcriptomic/proteomic/epigenetic states
   - MOFA Factor2 captures this dominant axis of variation

5. SIMPLICITY
   - Shows one plot, one factor (Factor2), perfect clarity
   - Any viewer can immediately see subtype separation


WHY OTHER PLOTS ARE SECONDARY
──────────────────────────────

R2 Heatmap:
  - Validates that MOFA can reconstruct omics data, not that it captures subtypes
  - A good model could capture omics structure without subtype stratification

Confusion Matrix:
  - Proves MOFA factors contain classifiable subtype information
  - BUT requires training a secondary classifier (logistic regression)
  - Does not visually demonstrate the stratification itself
  - Overall 82.8% accuracy is good but not as compelling as seeing 
    η² = 0.74 in a single plot

Ranked Weights:
  - Identifies driver genes (biological interpretation)
  - Does not directly show subtype separation
  - More relevant for "what genes define Factor2?" not "does MOFA capture subtypes?"


CONCLUSION
──────────
The Factor Boxplots plot provides the most direct, visually compelling, and 
statistically strong evidence that MOFA has learned a latent representation 
organized by breast-cancer subtype biology, particularly through Factor2's 
74%-explained variance and clear Basal-vs-Luminal stratification.
